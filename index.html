About Us

Sympathy Rwanda is a Rwandan organization committed to promoting and protecting children's rights while empowering families, young people, women, and communities to create safer and more inclusive environments for children.


Through advocacy, education, skills development, community engagement, and family training, we work with communities to address challenges affecting children and vulnerable groups. We believe that lasting change happens when children are protected, families are empowered, and communities have the knowledge and resources to take action.


Our work focuses on creating opportunities, raising awareness, strengthening families, and supporting communities to build a future where every child can grow up safe, respected, educated, and empowered.


Mission

To promote and protect children's rights while empowering families and communities through education, advocacy, skills development, and meaningful community engagement.


Vision

A Rwanda where every child is protected, respected, empowered, and given the opportunity to reach their full potential.
